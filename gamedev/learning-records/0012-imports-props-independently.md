# Uses the Blender → Godot pipeline on his own

Between lessons, and without being asked, he exported his Blender props (campfire, chest and others) and placed them in his Godot game "to see them there". So the Lesson 9 pipeline (origin, apply transforms, selected-only export, Apply Modifiers, import, a scene with collision) is now a skill he uses on his own initiative, not just something he was taught.

**Evidence**: His statement before L13.

**Implications**: Future lessons can say "bring it into Godot as usual" without steps. Lesson 15 (Godot) should build on this: level layout, gameplay or game feel, not importing. It also confirms that seeing his own work in his own game is a strong motivator.
