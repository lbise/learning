# Step-by-step lessons are under-challenging; switching to spec-driven challenges

He finished Lesson 3 (coins, signals, HUD) and said it was "still quite easy". That's the third lesson in a row reported as easy. Following numbered steps builds fluency, not storage strength: it shows he can follow, not that he can build unaided. From Lesson 4 on, lessons give concepts plus a spec with acceptance criteria, progressive hidden hints, and a full solution at the end, with stretch goals for the engineer (e.g. exact exponential smoothing).

**Evidence**: Self-report only. No quiz scores or kid-playtest feedback yet. Asked three times; ease off asking and rely on the spec criteria and his questions instead.

**Implications**: If he passes Lesson 4's spec without hints, raise the challenge again (smaller specs, more independent design). If he needs every hint, keep the format but shrink the scope.
